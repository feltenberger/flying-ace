# **FLYING ACE**

**Players:**

* **Standard Game:** 2+ Players  
* **With Alliances:** 4+ Players

## **A. Setup Rules**

* Each player starts with **4 Planes** & **32 Fuel**.  
* Play starts with the younger player and then goes counter-clockwise.

**Optional: Alliance Rules**

*(Only for games with 4+ players)*

* To create an alliance at any point: shake hands.  
* To break an alliance: 1 player must say "Unalliance \[Alliance Name\]" one time.  
* The last person or alliance standing wins.

## **B. Game Play Rules**

**ON YOUR TURN:**

### **1\. Roll the Die**

Depending on what you roll, follow these instructions:

* **\[ 1 \]** One of your planes is shot down.  
* **\[ 2 \]** Have a **Dog Fight**.
  * *Dog Fight Rules:*
    * A) Pick someone to fight against.
    * B) Roll the die (only the attacker rolls).
      * If you roll **1-3**: You lose a plane **and 10 Fuel**.
      * If you roll **4-6**: They lose a plane **and 10 Fuel**.  
* **\[ 3, 4, or 5 \]** Take **\[\# roll\] x \[\# planes you have\]**.  
  * *Example:* If you rolled a 5 and have 2 planes, take 10 Fuel.  
* **\[ 6 \]** You get **10 x \[\# planes\]**.

### **2\. Go to the Shop**

* You can buy a **Maximum of 1 item** at the shop.  
* OR you can choose not to buy anything.

### **3\. Pay Your Fuel Tax**

* Cost: **4 Fuel per Plane**.  
* If you don't have enough fuel, you will pay what you can and you will **lose a maximum of one plane**.  
* After you pay fuel tax, your turn ends. Play moves to the player on your left.
